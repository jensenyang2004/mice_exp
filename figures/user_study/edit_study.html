Multi-Object Edit Study

What you will do

Each screen shows an original photo, the list of objects that were replaced in it, and two edited versions, A and B, made by two different methods. The order and sides are random.

  • Natural: which edit fits the scene more naturally? Think lighting, shadows, scale, perspective, focus, and whether objects look pasted in or blended into each other.
  • Instruction: which edit better carries out the listed replacements, with every object becoming its own target?

Pick one for each question, even when they are close. Click any image to see it full size. There are 55 screens, about 10 to 15 minutes. You can stop and come back; your answers are kept.
